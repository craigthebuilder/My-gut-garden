Relish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Relish is a gut-health companion app that helps you eat a wider variety of plants by photographing your meals and growing a virtual garden. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the controls you have. The short version: your data exists to power your own experience, it is never sold, and you can delete any of it — or your whole account — at any time, right in the app.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address (or Apple ID sign-in), used only to operate your account.
  • Profile basics — height, weight, age, sex, and activity level, if you provide them. These derive your personalized daily fiber goal. They are never used for weight-loss features, advertising, or shared with anyone.
  • Meal photos — photos you choose to take are stored in a private bucket only your account can access. They are kept so your history and garden keep working, and you can delete any photo, any time.
  • Food logs and check-ins — the foods recognized in your photos, portions you adjust, and any optional wellness check-ins (for example mood or comfort notes) you decide to log.

How photo recognition works

When you snap a meal, the photo is sent securely to our recognition service, which uses Anthropic's Claude API to identify the foods in the image. The photo is processed to produce a food list and is not used by Anthropic to train models. All nutrition values come from our own food database, not from the image model.

What we never do

  • No selling or renting your data. Ever.
  • No advertising and no third-party ad trackers.
  • No sharing your photos or logs with other users or outside services, except the recognition processing described above.
  • No medical diagnosis. Relish is a wellness app, not a medical device — it never diagnoses conditions and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted on AWS, US East region) with row-level security so each account can only ever read its own rows. Transport is encrypted (HTTPS) end to end.

Your controls

  • Delete a photo — from any meal's edit view.
  • Delete your account — in the app under You → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, photos, logs, and check-ins. It cannot be undone.
  • Skip tracking — check-ins are optional and customizable, and you can turn the daily prompt off entirely.

Children

Relish is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here with a new date.
