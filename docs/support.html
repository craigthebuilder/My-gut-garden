Common questions

A food wasn't recognized correctly. Tap the meal to open the editor — you can adjust portions, remove items, or add anything the camera missed. Every correction makes recognition better.

How do I delete a photo? Open the meal from Today and use the delete option in the edit view.

How do I delete my account? Go to the You tab → Delete account. This permanently removes everything and cannot be undone.

Is Relish medical advice? No. Relish is a wellness app — it never diagnoses, and it's not a substitute for advice from your doctor or a registered dietitian.
