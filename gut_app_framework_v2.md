# Gut Health App — Product Framework v2.0

---

## 0. The one idea everything hangs on

The prebiotic fibers that make your gut **thrive** are largely the **same molecules** as the FODMAPs that **trigger** IBS — fructans (inulin/FOS), GOS, and some resistant starch are simultaneously the top bacterial fuels and the top IBS triggers.

- **Thrive** feeds them in.
- **Survive** pulls them out, then reintroduces them one group at a time.

One molecular truth through two lenses. The journey *is* teaching your gut to tolerate the fibers that make it thrive.

**Opposite visibility philosophies — and both are honest for the same reason:**
- **Thrive renders the invisible.** You can't see your good bacteria, so we build a garden you feed.
- **Survive hides the visible.** Symptoms go in, insights come out — no bacteria shown, because a "bad-bug meter" would be both unmeasurable from a photo *and* a shame mechanic.

---

## 1. Modes & the journey

| | **Survive** | **Thrive** |
|---|---|---|
| **For** | People with active gut distress | People who feel fine and want to optimize |
| **Engine** | Relief — find triggers, reduce symptoms, graduate | Curiosity — diversity, discovery, collection |
| **Framing** | Restrictive but *temporary* | Additive and aspirational |
| **Business role** | Retention + word-of-mouth (the moat) | Acquisition + broad funnel (the wedge) |

### The bridge
- **Graduation is a ceremony.** Survive → Thrive completion blooms the garden; confirmed-safe foods flood the Thrive pokédex. The celebrated, guided path.
- **Bidirectional and blameless.** A Thrive user who flares drops back to Survive — "let's go back to basics for a bit," never a demotion.
- **User-initiated switching, always available.** The switch to either mode can be triggered by the user at their own discretion at any time — held back only by a relevant pop-up disclaimer and **"Click to confirm"** functionality. Graduation is the guided route; manual switch is always one tap (plus confirm) away.

## 2. Onboarding & intake

**Lead with Thrive's fun** — the broad, shareable hook. Don't open with "how's your gut?" (reads medical, scares installs).

**Capture at intake:**
- **User goals** — e.g. reduce bloating, eat more diversity, fix IBS, more energy. Feeds both personalization *and* soft routing (relief goals → suggest Survive; optimization goals → Thrive).
- **Height & weight** — for personalization, fiber goals, and baseline only. *Per the disordered-eating duty of care (§10), never surfaced as a weight-loss or calorie-deficit frame.*
- **Baseline mood / energy / clarity** — so the "Is it working?" dashboard (§6) has a *before*.

**Social proof:** show other users' success stories at intake — builds trust and motivates. *Must be truthful and representative, not cherry-picked medical claims.*

**Disclaimers, not exclusion — nobody is locked out:**
- The app does not exclude anyone. Where a condition matters, a **disclaimer + click-to-confirm** does the work, then the user proceeds.
- **Survive intake surfaces disclaimers for the serious stuff (celiac / IBD)** and asks the user to acknowledge, rather than gating them out.
- **Red-flag symptoms** (blood, unexplained weight loss, etc.) trigger a "please also see a doctor" recommendation — a disclaimer and care prompt, not a block.

**Soft routing** by goal and symptoms — a suggestion, never a hard gate.

---

## 3. Progressive depth (serves mainstream *and* power users)

- **Tier 1 — everyone:** one snap → a couple of insights → one fun fact. Dead simple.
- **Tier 2 — unlockable, staggered:** the nerd pokédexes, one depth layer at a time, *earned*. The guild districts (§4) unlock in sequence.
- Gate nothing behind complexity; hide nothing from the curious. Tease the depth so QS users dig down before they churn.

## 4. The Pokédex Stack

| Pokédex | Tier | Scope | Measures | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Plant** | 1 | Both | Variety | 30/week, presence-based, rarity tiers |
| **Rainbow** | 1 | Both | Color (polyphenol/carotenoid proxy) | Eat-the-rainbow |
| **Microbial Guild Garden** | 2 | **Thrive only** | *Feeding* beneficial bacteria | Volume × frequency, **bloom** when fed |
| **Phytochemical** | 2 | Both | Compound classes collected | Food-ID → database lookup |
| **Fermented Finds** | Cross | Both | Probiotic intake | Daily tally + nudge; *celebrated in Thrive, cautioned in Survive* |

**Key distinction, made legible in-app:** the Plant pokédex measures **variety** (one garlic counts) and the Guild garden measures **feeding** (one garlic barely moves it). Different units, not a bug.

### The Microbial Guild Garden — full roster *(Thrive only)*
Four districts, unlocked in sequence. Confidence tags: **[solid] / [maturing] / [frontier]**.

**District 1 — The Backbone District** *(core fermenters; everyone hosts these, fed daily; unlocks first)*
- **The Anti-inflammatory Arsenal** — butyrate producers (*Faecalibacterium, Roseburia*). Feed: resistant starch, pectin, beta-glucan, oats, beans. → Fuels the gut lining, calms inflammation. **[solid]** *The backbone.*
- **The Appetite Crew** — propionate producers (*Bacteroides*). Feed: beta-glucan, barley, arabinoxylan, seaweed. → Satiety, steadier blood sugar, lower cholesterol. **[solid]**
- **The Base Layer** — Bifidobacteria. Feed: inulin, FOS, GOS (onion, garlic, chicory, legumes). → Acidify the gut, crowd out pathogens, feed everyone else. **[solid]** *Base of the pyramid.*
- **The Recycling Engine** — lactate utilizers (*Anaerostipes, E. hallii*). Feed: indirect — they eat what The Base Layer makes and turn it into more butyrate. → Stability; prevents lactate build-up. **[solid]**

**District 2 — The Keystones** *(small crews, outsized leverage; unlocks second)*
- **The Locksmith** — *Ruminococcus bromii*. Feed: resistant starch. → Cracks open RS particles so everyone else can feed; without it, RS passes straight through. **[solid]** *Discovery angle: some people host little of it and ferment RS poorly.*
- **The Knights of the Wall** — *Akkermansia muciniphila*. Feed: polyphenols (cranberry, pomegranate, green tea, concord grape). → Maintains the mucus barrier; tracks with metabolic health. **[maturing]**

**District 3 — The Scientists** *(crews that manufacture things for you; unlocks third)*
- **The Vitamin Lab** — vitamin synthesizers (→ K2, folate, biotin, B12). Feed: overall **diversity** — this is where your 30-plants number cashes out into a mechanism. → Micronutrient production. **[solid fact; diversity link is community-level, framed as such]**
- **The Mood Regulators** — tryptophan/indole producers, gut-brain axis. Feed: tryptophan-rich foods (oats, seeds, soy) + fiber. → Neuroactive metabolites; ties to the mood dashboard. **[frontier — real field, food→mood causality unproven; flag clearly or cut]**

**District 4 — The Hidden Gems** *(the endgame — not "feed a crew everyone has" but "find which crews **you** host"; rare, personal, stable traits; unlocks last)*
- **The Estrogen Regulators** — equol producers (from soy isoflavones). ~1 in 3 people host them. → Equol is a potent phytoestrogen (cardiovascular, bone, menopause). **[emerging]**
- **The Mitochondria Boosters** — urolithin producers (pomegranate, walnuts, berries). Metabotypes A/B/0. → Urolithin A supports mitochondrial and muscle health. **[emerging — early human trials]**
- **The Tumor Preventors** — enterolignan producers (flax, sesame, rye). → Enterolactone; hormonal and cardiovascular interest. **[associational — not proven prevention; see naming-claim risk in §10]**
- **The Stone Breakers** *(optional / advanced)* — oxalate degraders (*Oxalobacter*). Niche: matters mainly for kidney-stone formers; clean food→function link. **[solid but niche]**

**The honest catch on The Hidden Gems:** you can't confirm a metabotype from a photo of soy — whether you actually make equol is settled by a urine test after a challenge, not by tracking intake. So these are framed as *"a hidden trait ~1 in 3 people have — track your intake, and here's how you'd find out for sure,"* with optional routing to a test. The stickiest hook in the app (a trait you uncover about yourself) without claiming the app knows what it can't measure.

## 5. The supporting science

### Fiber → guild map
| Fiber | Feeds | Common sources | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inulin / FOS | The Base Layer | Onion, garlic, asparagus, chicory | Also a **FODMAP trigger** |
| GOS | The Base Layer | Legumes, some dairy | Also a **FODMAP trigger** |
| Resistant starch (RS2/RS3) | The Anti-inflammatory Arsenal (via The Locksmith) | Green banana, cooled rice/potato | RS3 = the cooled-rice fact |
| Pectin | The Anti-inflammatory Arsenal | Apple, citrus, carrot | Gentle |
| Beta-glucan | The Appetite Crew | Oats, barley, mushrooms | — |
| **Arabinoxylan** | Base Layer + Appetite Crew | Whole wheat, rye, bran | *Commonly overlooked* |
| Psyllium | (mechanical) | Husk | Poorly fermented — bulks, doesn't feed much |
| Mucilages | The Anti-inflammatory Arsenal | Chia, flax, okra | Fermentable |

**Fiber types beyond the obvious:** GOS, FOS (distinct from inulin), arabinoxylan, and RS subtypes (RS1–RS5; RS3 is the retrograded/cooled one).

### The volume problem — and its honest ceiling
- **Presence** (Plant pokédex): even one exposure counts toward diversity. Simple and correct.
- **Feeding** (Guild garden): scaled by **estimated portion × frequency**; gardens **bloom** with sustained intake. One clove = a small contribution, not permanent credit.
- **Getting volumes from a photo:** estimate *visible* portion into **coarse tiers** (trace / serving / lots), lean generous. **Hard ceiling:** the camera can't see garlic *in the sauce* — visible portion ≠ total intake. The feeding model is **directional, not precise**, says so, and lets the user confirm hidden ingredients ("onion in this?"). Never claim "12.3g inulin."

### Phytochemical taxonomy (Tier 2)
Most are **invisible to a camera**, so credited via **food-ID → database**: carotenoids, polyphenols, organosulfur, terpenes, phytosterols, saponins, alkaloids, chlorophylls, betalains. Color-mapped ones also feed the Tier-1 Rainbow tracker.

### Fermented foods (the Probiotic axis)
- **Add** bacteria (vs prebiotics, which *feed* existing ones). Strongest causal data of the three P's (↑diversity, ↓inflammation).
- A **daily tally + nudge**, not a snap-hook — most meals contain none, and that's fine.
- **Cross-mode rule:** *celebrated in Thrive*; *cautioned/testable in Survive* (some ferments are high-FODMAP or high-histamine and can trigger).

## 6. THRIVE spec

**Daily goals:** certain grams of fiber per day depending on height and weight (14g fiber/1000kcal ; DV kcal estimated based on height and weight) · 30 plant counts per day · eat the rainbow every day / every week (which colors are missing, which colors are weak (low quantity) - can click in to understand what each color means and what it does for gut)

**Per-photo daily view:** fiber count + plant count + new discoveries ("6 plants — 2 new: sumac, nutmeg!") · which P's this meal hit · rainbow contribution · *[Tier 2]* guilds fed / phytochemicals collected · one curiosity fact (variable reward). **Medical/allergy flag fires here too**, mid-celebration if needed (§9).

**Pokédex views:** Plant Garden · Rainbow · *[T2]* Guild Garden (the four districts) · *[T2]* Phytochemical · Fermented Finds.

**"Is it working?" dashboard:** mood / energy / clarity tracked against the onboarding baseline — the retention engine for users with no symptoms to chase.

**Notifications:** "27/30 plants — 3 to go before Sunday resets" · "Your Anti-inflammatory Arsenal is hungry — feed it some resistant starch" (gain-framed) · "Prebiotic + Polyphenol done — one fermented food completes your 3 P's" · rare-find and guild-unlock celebrations.

**Goals:** 30+ plants/week · 3 P's daily · keep your guilds blooming · complete the districts.

## 7. SURVIVE spec

**No guilds, no bacteria shown.** Symptoms in, insights out.

**Front end — symptom tracking only (~20 sec, evening):**
- **BSS** (Bristol 1–7, tap a picture) · bloating / gas / pain / urgency (quick severity) · mood / brain-fog · timing · food-correlation · context tags.
- **Gas-odor descriptor** — sulfur / sour / odorless. *The single most discriminating cheap signal, and the piece the logger was missing.*

**Per-photo daily view (food side):** FODMAP **safety check** (green/yellow/red vs Monash per-serving thresholds) · hidden-trigger flags ("this dish often contains onion — was it?") · during reintro: "contains [the group you're testing] — logging for your challenge."

**Backend — invisible pattern engine.** Over a few weeks, matches the symptom fingerprint to one of a handful of dysbiosis patterns: methane-leaning (bloat + constipation) · hydrogen-sulfide-leaning (sulfur gas, looser stools, worse after fatty meals) · hydrogen/SIBO-leaning (odorless gas + bloat) · fat-triggered · histamine (ferment/aged-food triggers, sometimes flushing/headache) · proteolytic shift (chronic low-fiber/high-protein).

**Output — insight → experiment → routing.** "Your pattern leans hydrogen-sulfide" → "drop these high-sulfur foods for ten days, we'll watch the signal" (structurally identical to FODMAP reintro) → "this is also what a breath test checks — worth raising with a GI." **Never a diagnosis, never a named bug, never an accumulating bad-guy meter.** IMO/SIBO/IBS-subtypes are medical diagnoses — copy stays pattern → experiment → confirm.

**The hook — reintro-as-leveling:** clearing a FODMAP group **unlocks** that food back into your garden — a visible win. Plus a positive **symptom-free streak**. Relief + visible progress *is* the engagement; restriction is never gamified.

**Pokédex views (foods, not bacteria):** Safe Foods (greyed-in as cleared) · Triggers (greyed-out, with severity) · Reintro progress (tested / passed / failed / pending) · symptom-vs-food timeline.

**Red-flag escalation:** persistent serious symptoms → "these warrant a doctor."

## 8. Symptom & lifestyle tracking

- **Burden is mode-dependent.** Thrive: one optional mood tap. Survive: the ~20-sec evening check. Default minimal; pain pulls people into more.
- **Odor matters** (Survive): sulfur vs sour vs odorless separates H₂S patterns better than anything else cheap.
- **Confounders matter.** Optional one-tap context at logging: *sick · stressed · poor sleep · traveled · new meds · menstruating.* The engine accounts for these so it doesn't falsely blame food for an illness- or stress-driven flare.
- **v1:** manual context tags. **v2:** Apple Watch / Oura / Whoop → passive sleep / HRV / activity, auto-context for "was it food, or did I sleep 4 hours and get stressed?"

---

## 9. The two-faced data model (load-bearing)

Applies to the **Thrive garden** and food flags. Every exclusion is tagged at onboarding:

- **Medical / allergy** (celiac, anaphylaxis): stays **LOUD across both modes**, including mid-Thrive-celebration in the photo view. Elevated hidden-ingredient sensitivity. Cost of a miss = harm.
- **Preference / intolerance** (onion gives me gas): goes **quiet** — silently omitted, no nagging. Cost of a miss = discomfort.

Same tile-greying in the UI; opposite behavior underneath. Flattening these into one "excluded foods" list is *the* bug to avoid — it would treat a celiac's gluten like someone's onion preference.

## 10. Open gates (before build)

- **Regulatory line — sharpest at the Survive backend.** IMO/SIBO/IBS subtypes are medical diagnoses; the engine must stay pattern → experiment → confirm, never "you have an overgrowth." This, plus the celiac flag, most determines wellness-app vs regulated-device.
- **Health-claim naming risk.** Several District 3–4 guild names — **The Mood Regulators, The Estrogen Regulators, The Mitochondria Boosters, The Tumor Preventors** — make implied health/disease claims that are **associational or emerging**, not established. Cancer-prevention claims especially are heavily scrutinized (FDA/FTC, app-store review). Likely need softening, a substantiation file, or in-app "[emerging science]" tagging before launch. Great Pokédex names; real compliance exposure.
- **RD in the loop:** reintro phase lengths, challenge sequencing, symptom interpretation are clinical — not improvised from a database.
- **Accuracy ceiling:** hidden-ingredient detection is inherent and mitigated, not solved. Design around "when unsure, flag it."
- **Disordered-eating duty of care:** restriction + tracking aimed at people who feel bad about food — no streaks on "days restricted," height/weight never framed as weight-loss, build the off-ramp in.
